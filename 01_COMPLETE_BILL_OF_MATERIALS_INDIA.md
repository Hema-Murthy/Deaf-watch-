## ⚠️ Important Shopping Tips

1. **Search Links, Not Direct Product URLs:** The "Where to Buy" links above are **search links** that will take you to the store's search results for that component. This is intentional — direct product URLs on Robu.in frequently break or change when products are restocked under a new listing.

2. **Check Multiple Stores:** If a component is out of stock on Robu.in, try these trusted Indian electronics stores:
   - [Robu.in](https://robu.in) — Primary recommended store
   - [ElectronicsComp.com](https://www.electronicscomp.com) — Good Waveshare stock
   - [Robocraze.com](https://robocraze.com) — Good for sensors & modules
   - [Probots.co.in](https://probots.co.in) — Lab-verified components
   - [Robobazar.in](https://robobazar.in) — Budget-friendly option
   - [Zbotic.in](https://zbotic.in) — Good Waveshare stock
   - [Sharvi Electronics](https://sharvielectronics.com) — GST invoice available

3. **Free Shipping:** Most stores offer free shipping above ₹999. Try to consolidate your order with one store to save on shipping.

4. **Out of Stock Items:** Some components (especially MB-102 breadboard, JST-PH connectors, TP4056) frequently go out of stock. If unavailable, use alternatives above or check Amazon India / Flipkart.
